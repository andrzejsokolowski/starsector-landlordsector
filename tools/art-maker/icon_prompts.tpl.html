Icon prompts / Landlordsector

Gemini prompts for the painted home condition icons: one per house trait, plus Residential area and Lore world. The grade and size icons are made by a script and are already done.

  1. New Gemini chat for every icon. Click Copy prompt and paste it; attach nothing.
  2. Save the picture into art-source/icons/ under the name on the card (png or jpg).
  3. Tick Done. Claude crops the middle square and shrinks it to 40 x 40, the size of the game's own condition icons.
  4. If it comes out too busy at that size, one short correction in the same chat: "simpler, one big shape".
