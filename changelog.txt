Version 0.2.2 (2026-11-12)
- Prices and location grades follow a new scale: hazard 100 percent is standard and 50 percent is ideal, size 5 and 6 are standard markets, 7 and 8 are big
- Pirate activity and Pather cells now lower prices, grades and security
- Location grades now show + and -, for example C+
- Comfort rises on low-hazard worlds and falls on high-hazard ones, with no extra penalty for barren or volcanic worlds
- Homes you own are revalued on the new scale

Version 0.2.1 (2026-11-12)
- The large picture on the property screens now lines up with the stat sheet, from the home's name to the end of the Security row
- Shorter rent line on the agent's screen, so it fits on one line

Version 0.2.0 (2026-11-12)
- Homes are now sold by property agents in market comm directories, not from a market menu option
- About a quarter of markets have an agent at a time (more on big markets), each stays 3 to 6 months; your own colonies always have one
- New property screen: homes listed on the left, the chosen one shown large on the right with its full stat sheet; the Manage page uses the same layout
- New stats: comfort, decor, tenant happiness, location grade and security
- Tenant happiness rises with comfort, decor, condition and cheap rent; happy tenants wear a home slower and tip more often
- Security lowers the damage raids and disruption do to your homes
- Decor raises a home's value and the reputation its rent earns or costs
- Cards name the planet type (for example arid world) instead of a climate word like hostile
- Escape now closes the Manage screen on the Real estate tab

Version 0.1.0 (2026-11-10)
- First release: buy homes from shacks to estates on markets that are not pirate or Luddic Path
- Prices follow market size, stability and the planet: mild, habitable worlds without pollution cost the most
- Rent comes in every month minus the local tariff, with five rent settings from Charity to Greedy
- Cheap rent earns reputation with the owners, greedy rent costs it and wears the home faster
- Homes wear out over about two cycles, are condemned when ruined, and are repaired with credits
- Sell any time from the Manage (Y) button, minus a broker fee and the tariff
- On your own colonies homes cost half with no tariff, and NPC buyers pay sales, property tax, growth and stability
- Raids and bombardment damage homes, saturation bombardment and lost colonies destroy them
