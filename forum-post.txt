[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Buy homes from property agents on the worlds you visit, from shacks to estates, and find tenants for them.
Rent comes in every month. Keep them in repair, set the rent, sell when the price is right.
Needs LunaLib and OddiszCommons.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Buying[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Every market of size 3 or more that is not pirate or Luddic Path has a property agent in its comm directory. Each lists 1 to 4 homes, more on bigger markets.[/li]
[li]The property screen lists the homes on the left and shows the chosen one large on the right with its stats.[/li]
[li]Bigger markets list more and better homes: shacks and studios from size 3, small houses from 4, houses from 5, villas from 6, estates from 7. New homes come on the market every 90 days.[/li]
[li]Base prices: shack 1k to 10k, studio 30k to 50k, small house 100k to 300k, house 500k to 1M, villa 1.5M to 2.5M, estate 3M to 5M.[/li]
[li]Market size, stability, the planet's hazard and nearby pirates or Pathers stretch those prices from a quarter to four times. Size 5 and 6 markets with 100% hazard are the standard. Low-hazard, habitable worlds with a mild climate cost the most, high-hazard worlds with pirate activity the least.[/li]
[li]Prices include the local tariff. On your own colonies homes cost half, with no tariff.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Stats[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Comfort: the tier's base, more on mild, habitable worlds, less on stations, hostile or polluted worlds.[/li]
[li]Decor: adds up to a quarter to the value and raises the reputation rent earns or costs.[/li]
[li]Tenant happiness: from comfort, decor, condition and the rent setting. Happy tenants wear a home slower and tip more often.[/li]
[li]Location: a grade from S+ to F for the market's size, stability, hazard, danger and conditions. Hover over the grade, or any stat bar, to see what makes it up.[/li]
[li]Market conditions from the base game and installed mods count: a Psychoactive Fungus cheers tenants up, a Rogue AI Core drags prices down.[/li]
[li]Security: from stability, military buildings and ground defenses, lower with pirate activity or Pather cells. Cuts the damage raids and disruption do.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Tenants[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]A new home stands empty. Advertise it and applicants turn up over the days, faster with a good location, comfort and low rent. Pick one of up to three.[/li]
[li]Each tenant has 2 or 3 traits that fit where they come from: pirates bring vandals, filthy tenants and late payers, Tri-Tachyon brings the prosperous and the tidy. You see one trait until you pay to screen them. A monthly screening service makes bad traits rarer.[/li]
[li]Each tenant's wealth (E to S) fits one kind of home, from a shack for E to an estate for S. Nobody moves into a home two tiers above their wealth or three below it, whatever the rent. Low rent makes a home easier to afford and high rent harder, by at most one tier.[/li]
[li]Homes roll 0 to 3 traits of their own: Leafy, Seaside, Scorched, Thin-walled, Geothermal and more. Tenants like or hate some of them: a pollen allergy and a Blooming home do not mix. Every home also gets a name, often from its traits. Hover over any trait to see what it does.[/li]
[li]Tenant events: tenants break windows, pay late, fix taps or buy furniture. Who they are, how happy they are, the rent and the home all count: a vandal on Charity rent behaves, the same vandal in a shack on Greedy rent wrecks it. At most one event per home every 5 days.[/li]
[li]Unhappy tenants move out. You can evict.[/li]
[li]Eleven headhunters, each on their own world (Chicomoztoc, Volturn, Sindria, Kanta's Den, New Auroria with United Aurora Federation, Eochu Bres, Mairaath, Port Tse, Kazeron, Gilead, Jangala). Each brings their own kind of tenants: cheap and hardy from Kanta's Den, rich from Eochu Bres. Hire them, send them on searches, and move the tenants they bring into your empty homes.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Rent[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Paid every month minus the local tariff, as Real estate in the income report.[/li]
[li]At Normal rent a home pays a share of its value each month: shack 3%, studio 2.5%, small house 2%, house 1.5%, villa 1.2%, estate 1%.[/li]
[li]Five rent settings from Charity to Greedy. Cheap rent slowly earns reputation with the market's owners and makes tenants happier, greedy rent costs both. A tenant on rent above their wealth is unhappy.[/li]
[li]No rent while you are hostile with the owners.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Upkeep and selling[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Homes wear out over about two cycles. A condemned home has no tenants and is worth only its land until you repair it.[/li]
[li]Raids and bombardments damage your homes. Saturation bombardment, or a colony that dies, destroys them. A market that changes hands keeps them.[/li]
[li]Sell any time for the value minus a 5% broker fee and the tariff.[/li]
[li]On your own colonies, NPC buyers buy the homes you leave: sale money, property tax, a little growth and stability.[/li]
[li]Everything is on the Real estate tab of the Manage (Y) button.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Settings and compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]LunaLib settings: rent multiplier, most properties you can own.[/li]
[li]Can be added to a game in progress. Removing it from a game in progress breaks that save.[/li]
[li]The house pictures are placeholders for now.[/li]
[/list]
