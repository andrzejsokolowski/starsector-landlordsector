[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Buy homes from property agents on the worlds you visit, from shacks to estates, and find tenants for them.
Rent comes in every month. Keep them in repair, set the rent, sell when the price is right.
Needs LunaLib and OddiszCommons.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Buying[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Every market of size 3 or more that is not Luddic Path has a property agent in its comm directory. A market has room for more homes the bigger it is (4 at size 3, 13 at size 6, 24 at size 8), and lists half to all of the free ones.[/li]
[li]Many core worlds have their own housing from their lore: Sindria's crowded hive cities, the Tri-Tachyon capital Eochu Bres from shanties to estates, the paradise world Gilead, the aristocratic estates of Eventide, unrest on Volturn and more.[/li]
[li]The property screen lists the homes on the left and shows the chosen one large on the right with its stats.[/li]
[li]Bigger markets list more and better homes: shacks, studios and small houses from size 3, houses from 4, villas from 5, estates from 6. New homes come on the market every 90 days.[/li]
[li]Base prices: shack 1k to 10k, studio 30k to 50k, small house 100k to 300k, house 500k to 1M, villa 1.5M to 2.5M, estate 3M to 5M.[/li]
[li]Market size, stability, the planet's hazard and nearby pirates or Pathers stretch those prices from a quarter to four times. Size 5 and 6 markets with 100% hazard are the standard. Low-hazard, habitable worlds with a mild climate cost the most, high-hazard worlds with pirate activity the least.[/li]
[li]Prices include the local tariff. On your own colonies homes cost half, with no tariff.[/li]
[li]About a third of homes for sale come worn, some dirty or covered in graffiti. They cost less, and repaired they are worth more than you paid.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Stats[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Comfort: the tier's base, more on mild, habitable worlds, less on stations, hostile or polluted worlds.[/li]
[li]Decor: from the home's size, its furniture and what tenants do to it. Adds up to a quarter to the value and raises the reputation rent earns or costs.[/li]
[li]Area: every home sits next to one building on its colony, or in a residential area. Mining next door is loud and dirty, the city center is close to everything, a resort is the best spot there is.[/li]
[li]Environment: Commute, Air and Noise, graded F to S+, from the planet, the area, the house traits and upgrades. Each home has its own spot on the colony, from the outskirts to the center, which sets its commute. An AoTD Maglev Central Hub gives every home on its colony an S+ commute. Climate: heat, cold and radiation.[/li]
[li]Each tenant has their own health and happiness. Health: bad air, heat, cold, radiation and a worn home lower it, some traits and upgrades raise it; sick tenants miss rent, end up in the hospital, and can die. Happiness: from the home's comfort, decor, condition, security and environment, the rent, and what that tenant likes or hates. Happy tenants look after the home and have better luck.[/li]
[li]Location: a grade from S+ to F for the market's size, stability, hazard, danger and conditions. Hover over the grade, or any stat bar, to see what makes it up.[/li]
[li]Market conditions from the base game and installed mods count: a Psychoactive Fungus cheers tenants up, a Rogue AI Core drags prices down.[/li]
[li]Security: from stability, military buildings and ground defenses, lower with pirate activity or Pather cells. Every tenant likes it; criminals hate police and spying buildings on their colony instead.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Tenants[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]A new home stands empty. Advertise it and applicants turn up over the days, faster with a good location, comfort and low rent. Pick one of up to three.[/li]
[li]Each tenant has 2 or 3 traits that fit where they come from: pirates bring vandals, filthy tenants and late payers, Tri-Tachyon brings the prosperous and the tidy. You see one trait until you pay to screen them. A monthly screening service makes bad traits rarer.[/li]
[li]Each tenant's wealth (E to S) fits one kind of home, from a shack for E to an estate for S. Nobody moves into a home two tiers above their wealth or three below it, whatever the rent. Low rent makes a home easier to afford and high rent harder, by at most one tier.[/li]
[li]Homes roll 0 to 3 traits of their own: Leafy, Seaside, Scorched, Thin-walled, Geothermal and more. Homes for sale may also come with an upgrade already done. Tenants like or hate some of them: a pollen allergy and a Blooming home do not mix. Every home also gets a name, often from its traits. Hover over any trait to see what it does.[/li]
[li]House events: tenants break windows, fix taps, paint graffiti or buy furniture, burglars break in, pipes burst. Who they are, how happy they are, the rent and the home all count: a vandal on Charity rent behaves, the same vandal in a shack on Greedy rent wrecks it.[/li]
[li]Tenant events: the tenant's own life, steered by their health, security and traits: a partner moving in, a baby, a pet, a promotion, a lost job, a mugging, a hospital stay, and sometimes death.[/li]
[li]Unhappy tenants move out. You can evict.[/li]
[li]Eleven headhunters, each on their own world (Chicomoztoc, Volturn, Sindria, Kanta's Den, New Auroria with United Aurora Federation, Eochu Bres, Mairaath, Port Tse, Kazeron, Gilead, Jangala). Each brings their own kind of tenants: cheap and hardy from Kanta's Den, rich from Eochu Bres. Hire them, send them on searches, and move the tenants they bring into your empty homes.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Family[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Declare one of your homes your own from its Tenants tab. You live there: no rent and no adverts.[/li]
[li]Propose to one of your officers or administrators of the opposite sex (the Your sex setting decides which sex you count as). Officers say yes at their highest level, administrators after governing one of your colonies for 12 months. They retire and live with you as your wife or husband.[/li]
[li]With AoTD Vaults of Knowledge and the Kaysaar's Demise setting on, Sophia Anderson can marry you once she is on your research council. She always says yes.[/li]
[li]Your spouse lives like any tenant, with their own traits, happiness, health and events. They miss you after 30 days without a visit (dock at that market), and an unhappy spouse divorces you.[/li]
[li]Talk to your spouse in that market's comm directory: try for a child, give a gift or ask for a divorce. Gifts are sold and turned into upgrades for the house. Every child at home raises the happiness and comfort needed for the next one.[/li]
[li]Married: no tariffs on that market, happier kids, more good events while you keep visiting. A divorce sells the house and gets you half. Your ex takes the kids and you pay alimony until they grow up.[/li]
[li]Kids are born with your name and Junior and grow up in five stages: Baby, Toddler, Child, Teen and Adult. They cost money while they live at home, and babies and toddlers wear your spouse out.[/li]
[li]From the Child stage, talk to them to steer what they become: an officer, an administrator, a miner who sends money home, a Nexerelin agent, or a local celebrity who makes a colony grow. As a teen they make up their mind for good.[/li]
[li]Their traits come when they start school, from the home (bad air, noise, commute or location, a small home) and from how happy the family is. Pay for lessons, spend days with them, send them to an Academy (Industrial Evolution) or pay for extra classes. They grow up with good or bad skills to match.[/li]
[li]A Romantic bedroom raises the chance of a child.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Furniture, upgrades and services[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Furniture: open markets on worlds with a property agent stock a few pieces, and salvage sometimes holds one. Right-click a piece in your cargo to put it in your furniture storage, then drag it into a home's slots on the Furniture tab of the Manage page, wherever you are. A shack has 1 slot, an estate 8, and a home holds one of each piece.[/li]
[li]21 pieces: a Sofa for decor, Cast-iron pans for health, Fans and a Space heater for heat and cold, an Air purifier, Grow lamps, a Fire extinguisher, a Safe, a Toy chest for families, a Grand piano and more. Hover a piece to see what it does.[/li]
[li]Upgrades are bought with credits (a flat fee plus a share of the value) and built at once, with no slots: Air conditioning, Better insulation, Soundproofing, Security cameras, a Fire sprinkler system, Lead lining, a Greenhouse, a Panic room, a Pool and more. Some raise the value by a percent, others by a flat amount. One-time jobs remove a Leaky roof, Musty mold, Freezing pipes, pests or asbestos.[/li]
[li]Services are paid every month as a share of the rent and can be cancelled at any time: a security patrol, a cleaning crew that removes graffiti and dirt, a maintenance contract that repairs the home, a gardener, a concierge, pest control and the screening service. The tenant manager costs a flat 5,000 a month and runs the tenants for you: it fills an empty home with the best applicant and moves the rent between Low and High to keep the tenant happy.[/li]
[li]Sell a home empty and its furniture goes back into your storage, or sell it furnished and the buyer pays for the furniture too.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Rent[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Paid every month minus the local tariff, as Real estate in the income report.[/li]
[li]At Normal rent a home pays a share of its value each month: shack 3%, studio 2.5%, small house 2%, house 1.5%, villa 1.2%, estate 1%.[/li]
[li]Five rent settings from Charity to Greedy. Cheap rent slowly earns reputation with the market's owners and makes tenants happier, greedy rent costs both. A tenant on rent above their wealth is unhappy.[/li]
[li]No rent while you are hostile with the owners.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Upkeep and selling[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Homes wear out over about two cycles. A condemned home has no tenants and is worth only its land until you repair it.[/li]
[li]Raids and bombardments damage your homes. Saturation bombardment, or a colony that dies, destroys them. A market that changes hands keeps them.[/li]
[li]Sell any time for the value minus a 5% broker fee and the tariff.[/li]
[li]On your own colonies, NPC buyers buy the homes you leave: sale money, property tax, a little growth and stability.[/li]
[li]Everything is on the Real estate tab of the Manage (Y) button.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Settings and compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]LunaLib settings: rent multiplier, most properties you can own (5 by default), cycles for kids to grow up, alimony per child, Kaysaar's Demise.[/li]
[li]Can be added to a game in progress. Removing it from a game in progress breaks that save.[/li]
[li]Every home size has two pictures for each planet type.[/li]
[/list]

[b]0.5.0[/b]
[list]
[li]Every home sits next to one building on its colony, or next to nothing. Mining or heavy industry next door makes it louder, dirtier and cheaper, the city center shortens the commute, and a resort is the best spot there is[/li]
