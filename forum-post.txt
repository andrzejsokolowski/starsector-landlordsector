[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Buy homes from property agents on the worlds you visit, from shacks to estates, and rent them out.
Rent comes in every month. Keep them in repair, set the rent, sell when the price is right.
Needs LunaLib and OddiszCommons.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Buying[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Homes are sold by property agents. Look for one in a market's comm directory. About a quarter of markets of size 3 or more that are not pirate or Luddic Path have one at a time, more on big markets. Each stays 3 to 6 months. Your own colonies always have one.[/li]
[li]The property screen lists the homes on the left and shows the chosen one large on the right with its stats.[/li]
[li]Bigger markets list more and better homes: shacks and studios from size 3, small houses from 4, houses from 5, villas from 6, estates from 7. New homes come on the market every 90 days.[/li]
[li]Base prices: shack 1k to 10k, studio 30k to 50k, small house 100k to 300k, house 500k to 1M, villa 1.5M to 2.5M, estate 3M to 5M.[/li]
[li]Market size, stability and the planet stretch those prices from a quarter to four times. Habitable worlds with a mild climate and no pollution cost the most.[/li]
[li]Prices include the local tariff. On your own colonies homes cost half, with no tariff.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Stats[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Comfort: the tier's base, more on mild, habitable worlds, less on stations, hostile or polluted worlds.[/li]
[li]Decor: adds up to a quarter to the value and raises the reputation rent earns or costs.[/li]
[li]Tenant happiness: from comfort, decor, condition and the rent setting. Happy tenants wear a home slower and tip more often.[/li]
[li]Location: a grade from A to F for the market's size, stability and planet.[/li]
[li]Security: from stability, military buildings and ground defenses. Cuts the damage raids and disruption do.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Rent[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Paid every month minus the local tariff, as Real estate in the income report.[/li]
[li]At Normal rent a home pays a share of its value each month: shack 3 percent, studio 2.5, small house 2, house 1.5, villa 1.2, estate 1.[/li]
[li]Five rent settings from Charity to Greedy. Cheap rent slowly earns reputation with the market's owners and makes tenants happier, greedy rent costs both.[/li]
[li]No rent while you are hostile with the owners.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Upkeep and selling[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Homes wear out over about two cycles. A condemned home has no tenants and is worth only its land until you repair it.[/li]
[li]Raids and bombardments damage your homes. Saturation bombardment, or a colony that dies, destroys them. A market that changes hands keeps them.[/li]
[li]Sell any time for the value minus a 5 percent broker fee and the tariff.[/li]
[li]On your own colonies, NPC buyers buy the homes you leave: sale money, property tax, a little growth and stability.[/li]
[li]Everything is on the Real estate tab of the Manage (Y) button.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Settings and compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]LunaLib settings: rent multiplier, most properties you can own.[/li]
[li]Can be added to a game in progress. Removing it from a game in progress breaks that save.[/li]
[li]The house pictures are placeholders for now.[/li]
[/list]
